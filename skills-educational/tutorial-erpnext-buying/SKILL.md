# ERPNext v15 Buying Training & Tutorial Skill

## 1. Instructional Objectives & Pedagogy
- **Target Audience**: Procurement Managers, Buyers, Purchasing Officers, and Supply Chain Coordinators.
- **Learning Outcomes**:
  - Understand the procurement lifecycle from Material Requisition to Purchase Order.
  - Run competitive bidding using RFQs and Supplier Quotations.
  - Track vendor performance, delivery reliability, and price variances.

---

## 2. Structured Curriculum & Lesson Plans

### Lesson 1: Requisition to Purchase Order
- **Material Request (MR)**: Originating internal demand from Stock shortages or Project needs.
- **Creating a Purchase Order (PO)**: Specifying vendor, payment terms, required delivery date, and target warehouse.

### Lesson 2: Supplier Bidding & Quotation Comparison
- **Request for Quotation (RFQ)**: Sending RFQs to multiple suppliers with one click.
- **Supplier Quotation**: Entering bids and using the Quotation Comparison tool to select the winning supplier.

---

## 3. Real-World Hands-on Scenario
**Scenario**: "The manufacturing plant is running low on packaging cartons. Issue a Material Request for 1,000 cartons, obtain quotes from 2 suppliers, select the best quote, and issue a Purchase Order."
- **Task for Learner**:
  1. Create a Material Request (Type: Purchase).
  2. Create Supplier Quotations for Supplier A ($1.20/unit) and Supplier B ($1.10/unit).
  3. Generate the Purchase Order awarded to Supplier B.

---
