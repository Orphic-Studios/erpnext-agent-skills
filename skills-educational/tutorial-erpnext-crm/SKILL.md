# ERPNext v15 CRM Training & Tutorial Skill

## 1. Instructional Objectives & Pedagogy
- **Target Audience**: Business Development Representatives (BDRs), Sales Leads, CRM Administrators, and Marketing Managers.
- **Learning Outcomes**:
  - Capture and qualify incoming Leads from various marketing channels.
  - Track Deals through structured sales pipeline stages in Opportunity.
  - Calculate pipeline metrics: win rates, deal velocity, and conversion ratios.

---

## 2. Structured Curriculum & Lesson Plans

### Lesson 1: Inbound Lead Capture & Qualification
- **Lead Creation**: Manual entry, CSV import, or web form integration.
- **Lead Status Progression**: `Lead` -> `Open` -> `Replied` -> `Opportunity` / `Lost`.
- **Assignment Rules**: Auto-allocating leads to sales reps based on region or round-robin.

### Lesson 2: Managing Opportunities & Stages
- **Converting Lead to Opportunity**: Preserving communication logs and customer history.
- **Sales Stages**: Prospecting, Needs Analysis, Proposal/Quote, Negotiation, Closed Won, Closed Lost.
- **Forecasting**: Factoring Probability percentage into weighted pipeline forecasting.

---

## 3. Real-World Hands-on Scenario
**Scenario**: "A visitor inquires via the corporate website about enterprise software for 200 users. Log the lead, record notes from the discovery call, qualify the deal for $45,000, and schedule a follow-up demo."
- **Task for Learner**:
  1. Create the Lead document.
  2. Log an internal communication note regarding discovery findings.
  3. Convert the Lead into an Opportunity with Stage: `Needs Analysis`.
