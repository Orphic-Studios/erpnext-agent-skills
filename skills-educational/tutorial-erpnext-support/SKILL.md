# ERPNext v15 Support Training & Tutorial Skill

## 1. Instructional Objectives & Pedagogy
- **Target Audience**: Customer Support Reps, Helpdesk Managers, Technical Support Engineers, and Field Technicians.
- **Learning Outcomes**:
  - Triage incoming customer Issues from email, web, or telephone channels.
  - Enforce response and resolution Service Level Agreements (SLAs).
  - Manage warranty verifications and schedule preventative maintenance visits.

---

## 2. Structured Curriculum & Lesson Plans

### Lesson 1: Ticket Triage & SLA Timers
- **Issue Ingestion**: Auto-converting incoming support emails into trackable Issue tickets.
- **SLA Matrices**: Tracking First Response Time and Resolution Time indicators.
- **Prioritization**: Categorizing tickets (`Urgent`, `High`, `Medium`, `Low`) and routing to support teams.

### Lesson 2: Resolving & Communicating
- Replying directly to customers from ERPNext with email integration.
- Documenting root cause analysis and resolution notes before closing.

---

## 3. Real-World Hands-on Scenario
**Scenario**: "A VIP customer reports that their POS terminal is failing to connect to the database. SLA requires first response within 15 minutes and resolution within 2 hours."
- **Task for Learner**:
  1. Open the high-priority Issue ticket.
  2. Send first response email acknowledging the problem to stop the FRT timer.
  3. Log the technician's fix notes and resolve the ticket within the 2-hour window.

---

## 4. End-of-Module Quiz & Assessment
1. *What stops the "First Response SLA" timer on an Issue ticket?*
   - [ ] Changing the status to "Open".
   - [x] Sending an outbound email communication to the customer who raised the ticket.
