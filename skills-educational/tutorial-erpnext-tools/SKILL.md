# ERPNext v15 Tools Training & Tutorial Skill

## 1. Instructional Objectives & Pedagogy
- **Target Audience**: General Employees, Team Leads, Executive Assistants, and Department Coordinators.
- **Learning Outcomes**:
  - Manage personal and delegated action items using ToDo.
  - Schedule company and team meetings using Events.
  - Maintain private or shared collaborative knowledge in Notes.

---

## 2. Structured Curriculum & Lesson Plans

### Lesson 1: Task Delegation with ToDo
- Creating personal tasks and setting priorities and due dates.
- Assigning tasks to teammates directly from any document (e.g., assigning a Sales Order to a logistics clerk).

### Lesson 2: Calendar & Events
- Public vs Private events.
- Inviting colleagues and tracking RSVP status.

---

## 3. Real-World Hands-on Scenario
**Scenario**: "Assign a task to the finance team to audit travel receipts before month-end, attaching the policy PDF and setting high priority."
- **Task for Learner**:
  1. Create a new ToDo assigned to the accounting lead.
  2. Set due date to month-end and mark priority as High.
