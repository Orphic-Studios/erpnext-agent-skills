# ERPNext v15 Home & Workspaces Training & Tutorial Skill

## 1. Instructional Objectives & Pedagogy
- **Target Audience**: All ERPNext End Users, Department Heads, and Executive Users.
- **Learning Outcomes**:
  - Confidently navigate the ERPNext desk interface, global search bar (`Awesome Bar`), and notifications.
  - Customize personal Workspaces with relevant Shortcuts, Quick Lists, and KPI Number Cards.

---

## 2. Structured Curriculum & Lesson Plans

### Lesson 1: Desk Navigation & The Awesome Bar
- Using `Ctrl + K` or the Awesome Bar to navigate instantly to any document, list, or report.
- Understanding list views, kanban boards, report views, and form views.

### Lesson 2: Designing Custom Workspaces
- Unlocking workspace edit mode.
- Adding Number Cards (e.g., "My Open Tasks", "Today's Invoices").
- Adding filtered shortcut buttons to accelerate daily workflows.

---

## 3. Real-World Hands-on Scenario
**Scenario**: "A collections officer wants their Home workspace to display: a Number Card showing total overdue receivables, and a shortcut button leading directly to unpaid invoices."
- **Task for Learner**:
  1. Create a personal Number Card aggregating `outstanding_amount` where status is `Overdue`.
  2. Pin the card and a filtered shortcut to their workspace.

---

## 4. End-of-Module Quiz & Assessment
1. *What is the fastest way to jump directly to any document or report in ERPNext?*
   - [ ] Clicking through every menu in the sidebar.
   - [x] Pressing `Ctrl + K` to open the Awesome Bar and typing the document name.
